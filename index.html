I work on data and evaluation systems for frontier AI, e.g., benchmarks, scalable expert feedback, and post-training/RL feedback loops.

  • I did my graduate work at the Stanford AI Lab with Chris Ré, where I worked on multi-task learning, weak supervision, and long-tail evaluation.
  • Before that, I worked on the data engine for Tesla’s Autopilot.
  • I also co-directed TreeHacks, Stanford’s flagship hackathon.
